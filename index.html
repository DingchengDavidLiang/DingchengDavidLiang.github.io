How I think

Start with the problem. Stay curious long enough to find a better way.

I like getting close to the real work first — the drawings, constraints, people, tools, handoffs, and small frustrations that shape the problem.

Then I look for leverage. Sometimes the answer is better engineering. Sometimes it is a clearer workflow, a simple spreadsheet, a new process, a piece of automation, or just a better way to connect people.

The part I enjoy most is turning an idea into something usable, learning from how it performs, and carrying that lesson into the next problem.
