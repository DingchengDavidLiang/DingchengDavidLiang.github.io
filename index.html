Current focus

Owning the knowledge layer, not just renting the model.

I’m especially interested in systems where the durable asset is the accumulated knowledge, skills, examples, and decision logic — while the underlying AI model can be replaced as technology improves.

This portfolio itself is part of that experiment: a public layer connected over time to a private, evolving skill and knowledge system.
