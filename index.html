About

I like finding the system behind the task.

My engineering background taught me to break complex work into constraints, quantities, interfaces, and decisions. Estimating pushed that further: accuracy matters, but so do speed, consistency, and the quality of the workflow behind the result.

That is what pulled me toward automation and AI. I’m interested in building tools that do not just produce an answer, but capture the process, preserve useful knowledge, and make the next task easier than the last.
