A path, not a pitch

I didn’t set out to collect skills. I kept following useful problems.

Engineering taught me to respect constraints, drawings, quantities, and the consequences of getting details wrong.

Estimating made me notice something else: many difficult tasks are not difficult because the answer is unknown — they are difficult because the process is repetitive, fragmented, or hard to explain.

That is what pulled me toward automation, AI, and better knowledge systems. I’m still early in that journey, but I want to keep building things that make real work easier to understand and improve.
